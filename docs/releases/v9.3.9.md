# NOOP v9.3.9

A release about the two workout complaints that would not go away: sessions the app auto-started that
never stopped, and the sport it named being the wrong one. Both were fixed against real recordings from
a WHOOP 5 strap rather than synthetic guesses. Everything still runs on your own device, offline, with
no account.

## Fixed

- **Auto-started workouts that never stopped (#auto-stop).** When iOS suspended the app mid-session, the
  next launch restored the session but forgot it had been *auto*-started — so neither auto-end path
  would ever fire, and the workout stayed "live" indefinitely, in the worst case for days. The restore
  now carries the auto-started flag, and when the newest sample in the restored session is older than a
  short staleness window it finalises the workout at the last real reading instead of resurrecting a
  days-old snapshot as if it were still happening. (Shipped in the 9.3.8 build; described here because
  that build went out without notes.)

## Sports that change

Read this if a past or future workout gets a different label than before. The coarse sport classifier
was recalibrated against ground-truth-labelled sessions from a real WHOOP 5/MG recording, because the
device's built-in activity ticks turned out to behave differently than assumed.

- **Court sports are recognised as court sports.** Badminton and tennis were reading as a generic
  "workout" or as a walk. The classifier leaned on the strap's "walk" activity tick, which on this
  device fires on *wrist* motion, not leg gait — so a racket swing looked like walking. It now trusts
  that tick far less and leans on motion magnitude, heart-rate shape and how little you stand still.
- **Gym sessions read as strength, not a walk.** The motion tolerance for strength was widened to cover
  a real gym session's wrist movement, and a spurious "ski" match that used to steal gym sessions was
  ruled out for indoor use.
- **A walk reads as a walk.** Brisk walking reaches a higher heart-rate reserve than the old band
  allowed, so genuine walks were being pushed into other classes. That band was corrected.

## Known limitation

- **Pool swim can still read as a low-confidence walk.** On a wrist strap a swim trips the same
  arm-motion "walk" signal as walking and sits at almost the same stillness, so the two are genuinely
  hard to separate from one recording. Rather than force a guess that could misclassify real walks, the
  app leaves it low-confidence for you to correct. More swim recordings would let this improve later.

## Under the hood

The sport classifier's scoring was validated two ways before shipping: against the real labelled
sessions above, and against the full existing unit-test suite (1314 tests) to make sure the new
device-specific tuning did not break the idealised-device signatures the classifier was originally
designed around.
