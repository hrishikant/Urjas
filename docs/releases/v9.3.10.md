# Ūrjas v9.3.10

A Today-screen release focused on comprehension: turning three raw scores into a screen that tells you
what to do with them, benchmarked against how the best recovery apps (WHOOP, Oura, Garmin, Apple)
present the same data. Plus a real fix to workout auto-stop for sports that keep your heart rate elevated.
Everything still runs on your own device, offline, with no account.

## New

- **A daily headline above the scores.** The Today hero now leads with a one-line call derived from your
  readiness , push, maintain, take it easier, or rest , so the "what do I do today?" answer sits above
  Charge / Effort / Rest instead of being left for you to infer from the numbers.
- **"vs your average" on every score.** Under each of the three scores is a compact trend sparkline plus
  a tag comparing today to the mean of your own trailing history (`+N` / `-N vs avg`, or "in line").
  A score now carries meaning relative to a normal day for you, not just an absolute value.
- **Streak + affirmation.** Once you have a run of scored days, a day-streak badge appears, alongside a
  short encouraging line keyed to today's Charge. Small, positive reinforcement rather than a bare number.
- **Calibration banner for new wearers.** During the first fourteen days a "Calibrating: day N of 14"
  banner explains that the app is still learning your baseline and that scores sharpen each day , so the
  inevitably noisy first-week numbers read as expected calibration, not a malfunction.

## Fixed

- **Auto-stop for sports whose heart rate stays elevated.** Auto-detected workouts previously ended only
  once heart rate fell back below the elevated gate. For court sports and interval training , where heart
  rate can sit just above resting for twenty-plus minutes after you stop , that cool-down never happened,
  so the session ran on. The app now also ends an auto-started session once phone motion has been still
  for a sustained period while heart rate is only just above the gate, catching exactly the case the
  heart-rate-only rule missed. When no trustworthy motion signal is available (permission denied or an
  unsupported device) the behaviour is unchanged, so it can never end a session on absent data.

## Under the hood

The new Today logic (the daily call, the baseline comparison, the streak/affirmation tone, and the
calibration progress) lives in small pure functions with full unit-test coverage, and the motion-assisted
auto-stop was added to the existing live auto-start/stop decision core with six new scenario tests
(including the badminton "heart rate parked high" case). Validated against the whole existing suite:
StrandAnalytics 1332, WhoopProtocol 531, WhoopStore 336, StrandImport 217, OuraProtocol 159,
StrandDesign 36, plus 1012 app-level tests , all green , and the iOS app builds clean.
