# Ūrjas v9.3.11

A recovery release for the one gap in workout detection: a sport played while the app was suspended.
Ūrjas already detected workouts after the fact, but only when it also had phone-motion data, so an
activity you did with the app closed (and the phone left behind) was never found even though your band
recorded the whole thing. This release adds heart-rate-only detection and a confirm-first card so those
sessions surface, are analysed in full, and are saved only when you say so. Everything still runs on your
own device, offline, with no account.

## New

- **Heart-rate-only retroactive detection.** After your band offloads its stored heart-rate history on
  the next sync, Ūrjas now runs a second detection pass that finds a sustained-elevated-heart-rate bout
  from heart rate alone , no phone motion required. This catches exactly the case the existing
  motion-plus-heart-rate detector could not: a workout done while the app was asleep or the phone was not
  on you. A bout counts when your heart rate sat at least thirty beats above resting for ten minutes or
  more, bridging brief dips (a water break, a sensor dropout) so one session is not split in two.
- **"We found a workout" confirm card.** New sessions appear on Today as a card showing the best-guess
  sport, duration, time of day and a quick stat line (average / peak heart rate, strain, calories). You
  can Confirm to keep it, Change sport to pick the right one, or mark it Not a workout , which hides it
  for good. Nothing is saved without your say-so.
- **Full analysis on recovered sessions.** A heart-rate-only bout is scored exactly like any other
  detected workout , heart-rate zones, strain, calories, average and peak heart rate.

## Fixed

- **No double counting.** A recovered session is only added when it does not overlap a workout the motion
  detector already found or one you logged yourself, and dismissing a session keeps it hidden even if a
  later sync re-derives the same span.
